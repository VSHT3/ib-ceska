# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective and current IB students, their families, school staff, and community
members who need clear, current information about the MYP and Diploma Programme
at Súkromné Gymnázium Česká.

## Product Purpose

Provide the school's authoritative bilingual IB programme website. Visitors
should be able to understand the programmes, subjects, team, student work,
admissions process, and current school activity, then contact the right people
with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Modern, confident, and IB-professional. The site should feel academically
credible, welcoming to students and families, and grounded in the real school
through verified information and photography.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic education templates, decorative marketing copy, overly playful
school branding, and interfaces that obscure practical information behind
visual effects. Do not let motion, styling, or promotional claims undermine
clarity or trust.

## Design Principles

- Lead with verified, current school information.
- Make programme choices and next steps easy to scan.
- Show the real people, work, and environment behind the programme.
- Treat English and Slovak visitors as first-class audiences.
- Use motion to support polish without making content depend on it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep content fully usable without JavaScript, respect reduced-motion
preferences, maintain readable contrast and semantic structure, and ensure all
core navigation and actions work across mobile and desktop.
