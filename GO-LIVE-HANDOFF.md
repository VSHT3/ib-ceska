## Safe sequence

1. Ask the school/domain administrator for permission and access to change **only** `ib.gymnaziumceska.sk`. Confirm whether they have already chosen a Cloudflare-compatible custom-hostname solution and who owns the Cloudflare account. Record the existing `ib` DNS record and TTL before changing anything.
2. Choose the hostname setup **before** editing DNS. A Workers Custom Domain needs an active Cloudflare zone; simply pointing an external CNAME at `workers.dev` is **not sufficient**. The paid partial-zone route leaves Websupport authoritative but needs Cloudflare Business or Enterprise: [partial setup](https://developers.cloudflare.com/dns/zone-setups/partial-setup/setup/) and [Worker Custom Domains](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/configuration/routing/custom-domains/). The Free-plan Cloudflare for SaaS alternative needs a dedicated backing zone or a fully audited `*/*` route with exclusions, account permissions and payment information on file. Do not buy a plan or repurpose another site's traffic without approval.
3. Configure the chosen route **without** changing the school's `ib` CNAME yet. For a partial zone, activate the zone and attach `ib.gymnaziumceska.sk` as the `ib-ceska` Worker's Custom Domain. For Cloudflare for SaaS, configure and validate its custom hostname, fallback origin and safe Worker routing; do not attach a Worker Custom Domain to the school hostname in an unrelated zone. Coordinate ownership/certificate pre-validation and a rollback window with the DNS administrator. Only then replace the old `ib` DNS record with the target specified by that setup. Keep `workers.dev` working.
4. In the Keystatic Cloud project **`ib-ceska/ib-ceska`**, add the new `https://ib.gymnaziumceska.sk` project URL without removing `https://ib-ceska.vsht.workers.dev`. Cloud login must be authorized for the new origin. No GitHub App callback URL is needed for Cloud mode.
5. Check HTTPS and the certificate, `/`, `/en/`, `/sk/`, styles/images, school documents, navigation, canonical links, sitemap and social preview image URL. Check `/keystatic/` login and one real Save from the new origin, confirm its commit to `main` and automatic Worker rebuild. The existing owner has confirmed CMS works on the Worker address; this new-origin check is still necessary. Confirm the school homepage and mail are unaffected.
6. If the new hostname fails, coordinate restoring the recorded `ib` Framer DNS record with the administrator; the Worker remains reachable on `workers.dev`. Do not remove the old hostname or rollback option until the new route is stable.

The three old GitHub-mode Worker `KEYSTATIC_*` secrets have already been removed. The exposed `IB Ceska CMS` GitHub App client secret and installation **still need removal in GitHub** by the App owner; do that separately from the domain cutover. More detail: [`humans/DEPLOY.md`](humans/DEPLOY.md).
